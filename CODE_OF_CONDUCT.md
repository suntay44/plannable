# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting comments, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to xtiansun@gmail.com. Project maintainers will review and respond in a fair and timely manner, respect the reporter's privacy, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions or temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior they consider inappropriate.
